# AI Shared Core
Record purpose, provider/model, adapter path, env-var names, input/output contracts, structured validation, retries/timeouts, rate/cost controls, logging, privacy, prompt/version strategy, fallback, tests/evaluation and known fixes.

When one project gets a stronger reusable AI implementation, separate general logic from business assumptions and update shared/REGISTRY.md with source repo/path/commit. Never propagate secrets, private data or business-only prompts.
